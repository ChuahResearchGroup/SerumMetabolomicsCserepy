Code used to generate the results and figures for the manuscript titled: "Exploratory Machine Learning Analysis of a Targeted Serum Metabolomics Panel for Mild Cognitive Impairment"
Currently submitted and under review at Metabolomics (Springer Nature)

Data included:
ADNIMERGE: the ADNIMERGE dataset from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative with demographic information and baseline diagnoses of patients in the study
ADMCGutMetabolites: Metabolomic data as collected by University of Hawaii, Wei Jia et. al
ADMCGutMetabolites_Dict: HMDB and other identifiers of each metabolite, as collected by University of Hawaii, Wei Jia et. al
bl_progression_data.csv: The preprocessed data after exclusion criteria were applied.
